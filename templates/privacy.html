Privacy notice

What we store

Account data: name, email address, optional institution and self-declared occupation, a password hash (never the password), sign-in timestamps and when you accepted these documents.

Analysis records: every analysis you run is recorded in your history (the image file name you uploaded, the predicted class, the confidence and class probabilities, the time and the model identifier), and the reports you save keep the same details. Image file names can reveal personal information, so use neutral names.

Security data: recent sign-in attempts (email stored only as a hash, plus the network address) to limit password guessing; single-use verification and reset tokens (stored only as hashes).

What we do not store

MRI images are processed in memory and are not stored. We collect no patient names, birth dates or medical record numbers, and no analytics or advertising data.

Who can see it

Your analysis records are visible only to your own account. Administrators can manage accounts but are not given access to your analysis records.

Cookies

We use a session cookie to keep you signed in and, if you tick "Remember me", a longer sign-in cookie. We use no tracking cookies.

Your choices

You can delete your saved reports in the app. Ask an administrator to delete your account and the data linked to it.
